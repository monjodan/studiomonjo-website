The studio

Every place Robey visits becomes a notebook.

Folding the sheets, drawing the thread through the spine, checking a finished notebook: these are small, repeated actions, but they make the object personal.

The maker

I put my soul into each notebook, so you can fill it with your own.

My grandmother learned to write with a dip pen. I grew up with fountain pens. Later, I spent many years working in artificial intelligence. The tools changed, but the physical act of writing stayed with me: a pen, ink, paper, and the pause before a line.

Working among words that can be generated so quickly made me appreciate something else: taking the time to write by hand and allowing a thought to wander. Studio Monjo began with that feeling.

Jordan MonnetMaker and illustrator, Seoul
