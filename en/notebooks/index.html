Questions about the notebooks

Who makes Studio Monjo notebooks?

I’m Jordan Monnet, a French maker in Seoul. I fold, sew and check each notebook by hand in my studio.

What paper do Robey notebooks use?

Robey notebooks have blank 105gsm pages chosen for handwriting, including fountain pens. Removable lined and grid guide sheets slip beneath the page when you want them.

Where can I buy a notebook?

For delivery in South Korea, order through the Studio Monjo Naver shop. For an international order, message Studio Monjo on Instagram with your notebook choice and delivery country. I’ll confirm availability, shipping and payment details.

Can I order notebooks for my company?

Yes. Company editions start at 20 copies. Choose a white cover with your logo or an original Robey illustration, with your message inside. Share your quantity, timing and idea through the company enquiry form.
