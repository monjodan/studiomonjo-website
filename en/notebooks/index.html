The notebooks · at the bench

Every notebook begins at this bench.

Some have no fixed form: paper, size, surface and binding can all change. Others follow Robey through Seoul, one numbered scene at a time.

01 · One of one

The next notebook is allowed to change everything.

One became a blue cover with a piece of hanji stitched across it and a line of calligraphy. The next might change its paper, proportions, binding and visual language all at once. There is no standard notebook underneath. The object itself—not only its cover—is the one-off.

Paper
Hanji, translucent leaves, textured or drawing paper
Form
Pocket-sized, tall, wide or cut to an unfamiliar proportion
Surface
Calligraphy, painting, stitched paper or another experiment
Binding
Thread and structure chosen for this piece alone
Never a catalogue

These photographs are examples, not a range to choose from. The next piece may resemble none of them. Ask what is on the bench now, or whether another one is beginning.

02 · Continuing line

Other days, Robey goes for a walk.

A real place in Seoul catches his attention. I paint the scene, print it around a cover, choose its thread and sew a small numbered edition. This story is allowed to continue.
