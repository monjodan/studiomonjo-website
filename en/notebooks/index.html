The notebooks · at the bench

Every notebook begins at this bench.

Some are made as one-offs, with their own paper, format, cover and binding. Others belong to the Robey series, with a new numbered scene each time.

Studio Monjo, Seoul · painting, folding and sewing at one table

01 Made once paper, format and binding chosen for one piece 02 Robey in Seoul a numbered series of painted scenes

01 · One of one

Each one-off starts from a different idea.

I once made a small blue notebook with a piece of hanji sewn to the cover and a line of calligraphy painted on top. Another may begin with a particular paper, an unusual size or a different binding. I decide the whole notebook as one piece. There is no fixed base model.

Paper
Hanji, translucent leaves, textured or drawing paper
Form
Pocket-sized, tall, wide or cut to an unusual proportion
Surface
Calligraphy, painting, collage or stitched paper
Binding
Thread and structure chosen to suit the piece

A one-off in detail

Yeobaek

A tall notebook built around the Korean idea of space deliberately left empty. Textured white paper, rounded corners, white thread and hand-painted calligraphy all belong to this piece alone. A short reflection appears on the back in Korean, French and English.

02 · Continuing line

Robey notebooks begin with a walk through Seoul.

When a place stays with me, I paint it with Robey somewhere in the scene. The painting wraps around the cover, then I choose the thread and bind a small numbered edition.

On the walk

I notice a place, a detail or a brief moment.

Back at the studio

I paint the scene and place Robey somewhere inside it.

At the bench

I print the cover, sew the edition and add Robey's letter.
