With every Robey notebook

A card with Robey’s letter

Each notebook leaves with a card from its place, on the same linen as the cover. On the front: the painting, the day the notebook was sewn, and the maker’s red seal. On the back: Robey’s letter, in Korean. The letter in other languages comes with it, on a paper of its own.

I can read, but I cannot write yet.

I wonder what you’ll put in yours.

Every scene begins with a small moment Jordan saw in Seoul. He draws it by hand, one line at a time.

Inside every cover

Blank pages, sewn with one thread

The notebook is sewn through its fold, without glue, so it opens flat. We picked a paper that takes ink well. Lines are a choice: a guide slips under the page when you want them.

About this prototype
  • The series is the one the collection proposal of 16 November defines: Robey in Seoul, with Namsan, the fortress wall, the COEX library and Seokchon Lake. Petals, Tree and Balloon are shown below it as earlier covers: whether they stay on this page is Jordan's call.
  • The map: the coast of Korea is real (Natural Earth, public domain). The Han River and the old wall are sketched from their bridges and gates. Each pin is a cover, cut from Jordan's own painting. No sea is named.
  • To check on the map: the pin for the fortress wall sits on the Naksan stretch of the wall, which is my guess from the painting; the Korean name 한양도성; Dokdo and Ulleungdo are drawn, the land north of the border fades out.
  • The fortress wall: since 26 November its pictures are Jordan's own photographs from the shared drive (the three taken for the Gwanghwamun application in October), in place of the grey card made from the drawing file. Its pin on the map is cut from the close photograph of the cover. Its two lines of letter come from the English review text in the story file, which is a draft. Its price and size follow the proposal: Pocket, landscape, ₩21,000.
  • The letter: the old folded letter is gone from this page. The words follow what Jordan said on 26 November: a linen card as the base, Korean on its back, and the other languages on a different paper. The picture is the Jamsil mock-up of 16 November, the newest I could find; the newer mock-up should replace it. Whether the whole letter may be read before buying is still his to decide, so the back is shown only in part.
  • Robey's other words are the ones already on the live site. "The COEX library" replaces "The Little Library", as in the proposal.
  • My wording, to check: "Four places so far", the line under the map, "North of the river", "South of the river", "Before the map", "Three earlier covers".
  • 26 November: Robey is made in A6 (Pocket) only, so one size and one price are shown. The three earlier covers are a gallery: the covers themselves at one size, cut from the paintings, with no price and no link, as Jordan asked.
  • A showcase for now: each notebook shows its size and price, then links to the Naver shop, to our dates and to Instagram. The fortress wall has no Naver link, since I found no listing for it.
  • Photographs that would complete it: the fortress wall notebook on the navy cloth like the others, its thread, and open; Seokchon Lake and Namsan open and standing; each letter beside its notebook.
