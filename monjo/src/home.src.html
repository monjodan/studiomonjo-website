How the film moves:

About this prototype
  • The desk is a 3D scene, and so are the three pictures above: they are the same objects the film shows. The cover's linen, the walnut grain, the lamp's weave and the navy are cut from Jordan's own photographs.
  • Stand-ins on this page: the handwriting (a font, not his hand), the cover of the Papers notebook, the pen, the ink bottle, the bobbin of thread, the shape of the lamp.
  • The three other pages are made of photographs. Only this page has a film.
  • 26 November: the three sections carry the names Jordan gave: Robey Notebooks, Unique and Limited editions, Accessories and Unique Objects. The top bar shows them in full on wide screens and as Robey, Editions, Accessories on narrow ones, where the full names do not fit on one row.
  • Each of the three pictures above shows one object alone. The third is the sleeve: on 26 November it replaced Yeobaek on the desk, in the film too, which was rendered again for it.
  • The sleeve on the desk is a 3D model read from his two photographs: a pouch, a flap that comes round the left edge and lies over the front, a band under the flap. Its size is the estimate on file for the Pocket sleeve (110 × 156 mm), not a measure. It lies flat on the wood of the table, close beside the mat (he tried it standing, then half on the mat, and chose this). It is built as cloth, thin at its seams and fuller in the middle, but with straight lines: his word was that wavy edges look badly made. Its suede is a patch of his own photograph of the sleeve, with the photograph's light taken out. The mat is 44 mm narrower on the right than before, to give the sleeve its place.
  • The two standing notebooks are no longer in a row (his idea, the same day): Robey's is a little forward and turned to the visitor, the paper one further back and more closed, and both stand further to the right. Their pages now fan across the opening: before, they left a wide empty wedge in the middle, which he found stiff. The ink bottle moved behind the mat so it does not stand in front of the sleeve. On a phone the film is cut narrower, and the sleeve is only at its right edge.
  • Robey is A6 only and the paper notebooks A5 only, so “Inside every cover, the same notebook” now means the same pages and the same sewing, in two sizes: to check.
  • My wording, to check: “Three ways in” and the sentence under it in the film.
  • One scroll now takes the page to the next step: the words move with your scroll, the film plays that move by itself, and when your hand rests the page finishes the step. The buttons above this note switch back to the earlier way, to compare.
  • A showcase for now: nothing is sold on these pages. They link to the Naver shop, to our dates and to Instagram.
  • Not built: For companies, French and Korean.
