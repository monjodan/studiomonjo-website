Our story

Why we make notebooks

With a pen you slow down, and you spend some time with yourself. We make our notebooks with care, because your thoughts deserve it.

Taking the time

Writing by hand

We like taking the time to write by hand, and letting a thought wander.

In a notebook you can turn an idea over, cross something out, draw beside it, or leave a page unfinished.

In the studio

Folded and sewn by hand

Our notebooks are not all perfect, and that is what makes them unique.

The page

A paper that takes ink well

We picked this paper for handwriting, including fountain pens. It has feedback: your pen glides, but you still feel the paper. The ink dries slowly, so you can take the time to find your words.

We leave every page blank. Each notebook comes with two loose guides, one lined and one grid, to slip beneath the page when you want them.

The maker

Jordan Monnet

I’m a French maker in Seoul. I fold, sew and check every notebook myself, and I draw Robey. What interests me most is what happens after a notebook leaves my hands.

“I put my soul into each notebook, so you can fill it with your own.”
