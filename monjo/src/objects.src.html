Accessory · two sizes

Notebook sleeve

A sleeve made to fit our notebooks.

The fabric is a microfibre with a soft, suede-like nap on both sides. A sewing factory in China cuts it with a die, and each sleeve is sewn on a sewing machine.

  • SizesFor Standard notebooks (A5) · for Pocket notebooks (A6)
  • FabricMicrofibre suede, both sides · navy
  • Made inChina

Unique notebook

Mini notebook

A notebook at the size of a name card.

It is sewn on the side with three stitches. A line of small cuts runs beside the thread, so each page tears off cleanly. One cover carries a small embossed mark.

  • SizeAbout 90 × 50 mm
  • SewingOn the side, three stitches
  • PagesBlank, they tear off

Nothing says handmade. The name “Notebook sleeve” is a placeholder.

Which object he calls the mini notebook and which the mini block of notes is my reading: to check.
